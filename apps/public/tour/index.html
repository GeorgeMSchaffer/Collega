PRODUCT WALKTHROUGH

All five stops

01 / SEE THE WORK

Make every item's status visible.

Boards organize items into the status lanes your team uses. Filters and card details help people find what needs attention without losing sight of the rest of the work.

02 / KEEP THE WHY

Keep the reasoning beside the item.

Capture the problem, proposed solutions and impact rationale together. Comments, blockers and related work stay close to the context people need to make a decision.

03 / LINE UP WHAT'S NEXT

Give unscheduled work a home.

The Backlog gathers tasks that are not in a sprint yet. Review the work waiting, then schedule the items that fit the team's next stretch.

04 / COORDINATE DELIVERY

Give a sprint a shared focus.

See the sprint goal, time window and progress alongside work moving through delivery stages. The team can follow what is underway and what is finished.

05 / LOOK AHEAD

See the next sprint on the horizon.

The Roadmap places current and upcoming sprints on a timeline. Teams can look ahead while keeping delivery connected to the ideas that started the work.
