What you can do

  • Work by status

    Lanes group ideas by status so the team sees what is new, in review, in progress and done.

  • Linked tasks

    Link an idea to tasks on a work board. Tasks keep their own statuses and blockers while the idea keeps its context.

  • Open an idea in place

    Open any card to see its problem, proposed solutions, impact rationale and discussion.
