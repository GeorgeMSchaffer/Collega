A few things about the beta

Who is WorkSprint for?

Cross-functional teams managing improvement ideas and the work needed to deliver them.

Does signup give me immediate access?

No. We review beta requests manually and contact applicants about access. Submitting does not create an account.

Do I have to use the AI assistant?

No. You can fill out an idea manually. The assistant offers draft suggestions; you keep control of your edits and submission.

Can existing users still sign in?

Yes. Use Login to open the application. Beta requests and application accounts are separate.
