Merge duplicates

Merge two ideas into one

Everything from the merged idea moves to the one you keep. This is reversible for 30 days from the kept idea's history.

What happens
  • 2 comments move across, keeping their authors and timestamps.
  • 1 vote moves across. Maya voted on both — she is counted once, so the total becomes 2, not 3.
  • Followers and assignees are combined.
  • The merged idea's description is not merged — it is kept on the closed idea and quoted in a comment on the one you keep. Concatenating two descriptions produces something nobody wrote.

This is the whole argument for #6. One required field at the moment of closing produces a page that neither Trello nor Jira gives you: a searchable record of what your organization decided and why. This is the one proposal with a real security surface. An unauthenticated, tokenized URL is a new way for organization-scoped data to leave the organization, and the role model has no concept of "not a member" today. Four things this comp takes a position on, all of which need confirming before any of it is specified: the token is the only credential unless a passphrase is set; expiry defaults to 30 days rather than never; people and comments are off by default; and creating a link is Org Admin only, never a User. Rejecting any one of those changes the feature.

Relationship to the Read Only role. This is deliberately not the same thing. Read Only is a member of the organization with an account; a share link is for someone who will never have one — the client implied by the "Client Review" status. Conflating them would mean creating accounts for people you cannot onboard.
