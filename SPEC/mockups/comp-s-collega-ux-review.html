Field service enablement: Retire the legacy step

BoardOpportunities
Idea typeContinuous Improvement
AssigneeNoah Contributor
DueNov 7, 2026
Problem

Technicians still fax signed service reports from customer sites, and the office re-enters them, even though the tablet app captures signatures.

Proposed solutions

Retire the fax step after billing confirms tablet signatures are accepted. Remove the old template and tell customers signed reports now arrive by email.

Impact rationale

Re-keying takes the office about 15 hours a week and delays invoicing by two days on average.
