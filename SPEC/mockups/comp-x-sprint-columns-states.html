9Questions this comp cannot answer

Open questions for the owner
  1. A sprint past its end date: as drawn it stays Ongoing with an Overdue chip until someone completes it. Is there a Complete sprint action, and who may take it? I found none in the specs I read.
  2. Unfinished tasks when a sprint completes: the Completed card says "3 returned to the Backlog". Confirm that is true, or drop the line.
  3. May two sprints on one board be Ongoing at once? The page would list both; the Sprint board shows one.
  4. A Completed card: read-only summary page, or the board’s Sprints tab? Drawn as a read-only summary.
  5. How many completed sprints load first (drawn: 3, then 10 at a time)?
