The right-hand column is the argument for the whole feature: what the crowd wants that nobody committed to, and what shipped that nobody measured.

Approach D — Roadmap First. One new rail destination, and its landing page answers the question an org actually asks: are we delivering what we said we would? Sprints are visible as vertical boundaries crossing every roadmap — orthogonality is not explained, it is simply drawn. Upvote counts ride along on the bars, so crowd support stays attached to committed work.
Trade-off. This optimises for the stakeholder who visits weekly, not the assignee who lives here daily — the sprint board, where the actual work happens, is one level down. Expect “why do I have to click through a Gantt to get to my work?” Mitigation is a Sprints tab that remembers where you were, or a per-user default landing view.

Automate the new-hire equipment request

Originated as an idea by Maria Ortiz on Jul 2 · promoted by Jordan Reyes on Aug 8

New hires wait four to six days for a laptop because the request is a paper form that walks between three desks. Automating it against the HR record should collapse that to same-day.
