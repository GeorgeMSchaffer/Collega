Approach C — The Loop. One destination shows the entire life of an item, so nothing ever “disappears into delivery”. The promotion gate and the did-it-work gate are drawn as real boundaries, which is the product’s whole argument made visible in a single screen. The two-status problem is handled by collapsing: Discovery shows ideation status as a card subtitle, delivery status as a coloured band.
Trade-off — and it is a real one. The org’s configurable ideation swimlanes collapse into one “Discovery” column. An admin who carefully built five statuses sees them demoted to grey subtitles, and drag-to-change-status is gone on the left half of the canvas. This surface almost certainly has to supplement the board rather than replace it, which means maintaining two ways to look at Discovery.

Prompted at Complete, never required. Feeds the deferred reporting feature with the only data no other tracker has: claim versus result, on the same row as the original debate.

The follow-up idea is the loop closing twice. “Two of four managers still assign manually” is exactly the raw material for a new Discovery item — the outcome note is where the next idea usually comes from, and it starts life already carrying the evidence.
Trade-off. An Outcome stage on the canvas implies someone will fill it in. If adoption is low it becomes a visible column of blanks, which reads as neglect. A single “Outcome recorded” badge on the Shipped card may be the safer first cut, with the dedicated stage arriving only once the habit exists.
