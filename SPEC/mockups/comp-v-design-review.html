Comp V: design review recommendations

Five changes drawn from a walk of Home, Inbox, Boards, a board, Ideas, Sprint board, Backlog, Roadmap and Settings as an Org Admin on the Graphite theme (1440 wide). Each pair shows what ships today and the proposal. Nothing here is built or decided.

1Spend the amber on actions only

Amber is the primary button, every row link, every breadcrumb and the Home figures. Everything reads as clickable, so nothing leads. Proposal: amber for the one primary action per view; row titles in the text colour with an underline on hover; breadcrumbs in muted text. On Ideas, a row that wraps its title to three lines and carries a full "Remove from board" button on every row becomes a wider title and a row menu.

2A calmer card on the board

Each card shows priority, assignee initials, tags, an upvote count and two arrow buttons at once, in a column about 120 px wide. Proposal: key and priority on one line, assignee as an avatar, tags limited to one plus a count, and the arrows only on hover and focus (the keyboard path stays).

3Make Home lead with what is assigned

Four tiles share equal weight, one of them a dashed "Not tracked yet" tile of three dashes, and a permanent onboarding banner sits above them. Proposal: "Assigned to me" leads and carries the first three items inline; the untracked metrics collapse into a single footnote until the data exists; the banner becomes a one-line dismissible strip.

4An honest Roadmap before Outcomes ship

The Roadmap shows an empty grey timeline band, then two amber-looking "Add Outcome" buttons that do nothing, each followed by "Outcomes arrive in a later release". Dimmed primary buttons with an apology beside them teach people to distrust the buttons. Proposal: no dead controls; explain what Outcomes will be and point to what works today.

5Name what each lane hands work to (board form)

In the lane editor from slice 288 each lane shows a Normal / Final toggle and a bare select reading "None". Nothing says what the select does until you open it. Proposal: label it "Hands work to", show the reference pill once set, and reveal the auto-create switch beneath it only then, as comp U draws.
