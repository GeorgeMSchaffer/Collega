Before/after for the findings from the 2026-10-19 walkthrough. “Before” is a faithful sketch of the current screens at ~1000px; “After” is the proposal. Throwaway comp for review, not production.

1 · Sprint board — cards stay inside their lanes

Effort labels in mono overflow the card and lane. Proposal: a 3-segment bar with an H/M/L letter, card min-width 0, title wraps, assignee pinned top-right.

2 · Ideas table and inline add — full-width add bar, flexible title column

The add input lives in the Title column (~60px) with a 4-line hint. Proposal: a full-width add bar above the table (hint in a placeholder, shortcut chip at right); title column grows, secondary columns truncate; Tags and Board collapse under 1100px.

3 · Home — quieter onboarding, no “not tracked” tile

The amber banner competes with the greeting and the dashed “Not tracked yet” tile advertises a gap. Proposal: neutral one-line tip, three tiles that all carry data.
