This replaces the "Activity feed coming soon" placeholder. Two panels, deliberately different: Activity is everything that happened and is passive; Waiting on you is the subset addressed to you and is the actual to-do list. Mixing them is the common mistake — a feed that includes both becomes something people stop reading, and then a mention buried in it is worse than no mention at all.

Feed entries quote their content. "Maya commented" is not worth a click; the first line of what Maya said usually is. This is the cheapest thing that makes a feed read rather than skimmed.

Every row says why you got it. The small caps line ("mentioned you", "replied to you", "assigned you", "is stalled") is the notification's reason for existing, and it is text — never a coloured icon on its own. That line is also exactly what the preferences screen lets you switch off, so the two screens use one vocabulary.

Snooze rather than only archive. The stalled-idea notification is the one people archive to make it go away and then forget. Snooze keeps it honest.

Unread uses indigo — flagging this for a decision. The locked rule reserves indigo for "active / selected / primary action". The argument for using it here is that an unread notification is a pending primary action. If the reviewer disagrees, the fallback is weight plus a filled ink dot and a left rule, with no hue — it costs a little scannability and breaks no rule.

Email is the risky column. SPEC/00-project-brief.md defers guaranteed outbound email delivery, so the email switches here are a promise the platform has not made yet. Two honest options: ship the inbox first and hide the email column entirely, or ship it labelled best-effort. This comp shows the column so the decision is visible — it should not be built as drawn without that call being made.

Mentions are deliberately not fully switchable. Everything else can be silenced. If a direct question can be silenced, mentions stop being reliable and people go back to email — which is the behaviour this feature exists to replace.

You're all caught up

Nothing is waiting on you. New mentions, replies and assignments land here — and anything you archive stays available under Archived.

Worth designing on purpose. For a new organization this is the first state of the inbox, not an edge case — and a permanently empty-looking inbox is how a notification feature dies quietly. Naming what will appear here, and where archived items went, does most of that work.
