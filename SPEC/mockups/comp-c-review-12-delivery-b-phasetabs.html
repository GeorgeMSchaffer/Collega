Approach B — Phase Tabs. The rail never grows. Switching Discovery → Delivery is a lens change on the board you are already looking at, which is the literal UI expression of “same row, later phase”. Promoted cards stay in their ideation swimlane, muted, so the crowd keeps its view.
Trade-off. Roadmaps are org-wide outcome themes, but here they hang off a board — and a board is a configured set of swimlanes, a much smaller idea. A roadmap spanning two boards has no natural home. Sprint admin also has to live somewhere that isn’t “a board”.
