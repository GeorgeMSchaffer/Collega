Why this shape

An outcome is a grouping lens over issues, not a container that owns them. That keeps the spec's rule intact — an issue belongs to zero or one sprint — while still answering “what are we trying to achieve this quarter?”

Open question for you: should an issue be able to sit under two outcomes at once? Single-parent is simpler to reason about and to render; multi-parent matches how quarterly goals actually overlap.
