Then most tools lose it

Once approved, the idea becomes a card in an “Implementation” column. The plan stays in one tool; the work, if it is tracked at all, is somewhere else.

Consolidate regional warehousesImplementation since Aug 3 · 11 weeks

Is it on track? Blocked? Half done? Someone has to ask, and the answer lives in another tool.

Not here

In Collega, “Implementation” can open onto the work board. Each task has its own status, owner and sprint, and the total rolls up to the card.
