Questions

Quick answers.

Where do I sign in?

At app.collega-ai.com. This site is for learning about Collega and creating a workspace.

Who can see our data?

Only members of your organization. Each organization is a separate boundary.

Can the AI change our ideas on its own?

No. The assistant drafts a form; a person reviews and submits it.

How do we bring in our team?

Share an invite code, add people directly, or import a CSV.
