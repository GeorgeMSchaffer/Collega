How it works

From a hallway comment to a shipped change.

01

Capture

Anyone raises an idea — typed, or talked through with the assistant.

02

Sort

Ideas land on a board and move through your statuses.

03

Decide

Comments and upvotes surface what's worth doing.

04

Deliver

Promote to an issue, plan the sprint, track tasks.
