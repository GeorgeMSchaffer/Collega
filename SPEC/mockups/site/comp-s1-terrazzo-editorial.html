01

Ideas get lost where work already happens.

In chat, in meetings, in the margin of someone's notebook. Nobody knows what was suggested, what was decided, or who's on it. Collega gives every idea a card, a status, and a history — in a space designed for exactly that.

02

One place to raise, sort and decide.

Boards use your organization's own statuses and idea types. Comments, @mentions and upvotes show what the team cares about. An assistant can help someone turn a rough thought into a clear idea — and they stay in control of what gets submitted.

03

From “what if” to “it shipped.”

Approved ideas don't get re-entered into another tool. They become issues — the same item — and move through sprints and tasks.

Capture

Anyone can raise it

Users, admins and read-only collaborators each get the access they need — and no more.

Decide

Evidence, not volume

Upvotes, comments and tags show where the energy is, so loud ideas don't win by default.

Deliver

Traceable end to end

Backlog, sprint planning and a five-stage board — with the original idea one click away.
