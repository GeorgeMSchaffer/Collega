Four other ways to give tasks a home and fill a sprint

Comp W (call it A) is a Backlog tab per board plus a picker in the create-sprint drawer. These alternates keep the same data rules (a task is a card on a work board; a sprint holds one board's tasks; the picker lists unassigned tasks) and change where the work starts: the sprint, a global list, the board itself, or the parent idea. Sample data is Acme Robotics. Nothing saves. Strings marked COPY PROPOSED are not decided.

My read: A and D combine well (D is A's Backlog tab rendered as a panel instead of a tab). B is the strongest answer to "pick tasks when creating a sprint" if sprints are a weekly ritual. C is worth adding later as "My tasks" rather than building first. E is a good complement for idea owners but cannot be the only home, because it leaves standalone tasks out.

IMPL-40Kickoff notes

Drop a backlog row on a lane to put it in the active sprint and set its status to that lane (decided 2026-10-22); Add adds it to the sprint and leaves status alone. Blocked or locked moves are refused and the task stays in the backlog. New sprint opens the comp W drawer with its picker. The panel is the Backlog tab, always one glance away.

Idea: no new page and no tab to remember: the backlog sits beside the board people already use, like a tray. Trade-off: cramped on a narrow screen (it becomes a bottom sheet on mobile); adding a task straight into a lane is a second path to explain; and a drop changes status as well as sprint, so the Add button is the schedule-only path (status unchanged).
