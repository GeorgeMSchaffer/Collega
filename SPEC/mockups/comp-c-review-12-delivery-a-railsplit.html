Approach A — Rail Split. Discovery and Delivery are two labelled worlds in the 64px rail. Everything is one click from anywhere; nothing is nested under a noun it doesn’t belong to. Roadmaps and Sprints get top-level standing, matching how admins actually think about them.
Trade-off. The rail goes from 4 destinations to 7 plus two group labels — it is now the tallest element on the page and the visual weight says “this is two applications sharing a login.” That is precisely the impression the one-row-two-phases model exists to avoid. Also the first thing to break at narrow viewports, which are still undesigned.
