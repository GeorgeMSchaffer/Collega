# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Collega helps organization members submit, discuss, and track process ideas. Canonical product behavior lives in `SPEC/*.md`; this design context does not supersede it.

## Users

Organization members create and collaborate on ideas; organization administrators manage boards and configuration; Read Only users can inspect and follow ideas (`SPEC/20-feature-client-ui.md`, `SPEC/20-feature-idea-following.md`).

## Operating Context

The signed-in client offers boards, idea creation and detail, and an in-app notification inbox. The three requested review prototypes explore these existing flows using illustrative local data, not a backend.

## Capabilities and Constraints

These prototypes do not establish product scope or ship production behavior. Pricing, social proof and marketing CTA patterns from the supplied article are not in-product requirements. `SPEC/mockups/comp-r-portico-prototype.html` remains the reference; its inbox sibling is awaiting approval.

## Product Principles

- Make the next meaningful action clear at first use.
- Help people correct input before submission.
- Give accurate feedback without pretending local prototype state is server state.
- Keep role boundaries and accessible interaction intact.
